# Practical Digital Housekeeping

Keep your digital life in order, starting with your password database.

A method for maintaining the accounts, devices, identities, credentials, and records that make up everyday digital life.

**Keep it tidy. Keep it trustworthy.**

Website: **https://pdh.cprima.net**
